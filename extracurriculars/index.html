Extracurriculars

Taekwondo: For over four years now, I have been doing Taekwondo as my sport and physical activity of choice. After working my way up throughout my first three years of high school, I obtained my first-degree black belt in May 2025, and my second degree in May of this year. I am now more involved than ever as a training instructor, where I teach the 7-12-year-old students. I have also competed in eight Taekwondo tournaments, so far winning twice in sparring and once in forms. I plan to continue training and competing in the future, likely through UC Irvine's Taekwondo program in addition to with my studio.

Academic Decathlon: I competed in the TCHS Academic Decathlon — a competition between high schools involving examinations and public speaking on seven core subjects — in the 2023-24 and 2024-25 school years, when I was a sophomore and junior in high school respectively. I particularly enjoyed doing this because of the people that I met in it (who are dedicated to the program like myself), along with the practice in testing and public speaking that it gives me. I further practiced my public speaking in my senior year (2025-26) through presenting the program to other students in the school in an attempt to revive it. Though we did not compete as a result, I still hope that my advertisement for the program can convince some of the underclassmen to continue it after I am gone.

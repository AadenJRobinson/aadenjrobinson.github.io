Extracurriculars

Taekwondo: For over four years now, I have been doing Taekwondo as my sport and physical activity of choice. After working my way up throughout my first three years of high school, I obtained my first-degree black belt in May 2025. I am now more involved than ever as a training instructor, where I teach the 7-12-year-old students. I have competed in seven Taekwondo tournaments as of now, and will be entering more in the future when I have the chance.

Academic Decathlon: I have been a part of the TCHS Academic Decathlon — a competition between high schools involving examinations and public speaking on seven core subjects — since it was introduced to the school in the 2023-24 year. I particularly enjoy doing this program because of the people that I often meet in it that are interested in it as well, along with the practice in testing and public speaking that it gives me.
